C-section deliveries rose sharply, led by private facilities.

National c-section prevalence increased from 17.2% in NFHS-4 to 21.5% in NFHS-5 and 27.2% in NFHS-6. The facility split is starker: 54.1% of births in private health facilities were c-sections in NFHS-6, compared with 16.9% in public facilities.

Source: NFHS-6 2023-24 Fact Sheets. NFHS-5 values are the comparison columns printed in the NFHS-6 sheets. NFHS-4 national baseline values are from the India key-indicator fact sheet.
